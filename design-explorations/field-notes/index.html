Lunch / 12:30

Lemony chicken,
orzo & greens

42 g protein18 active min1 portion

Gather

  • 180 g chicken thigh
  • 90 g dry orzo
  • 1 small courgette
  • Two handfuls spinach
  • Lemon, yoghurt, dill

Prepare

  1. Brown the chicken deeply and set aside.
  2. Toast orzo and simmer with stock and courgette.
  3. Fold through spinach and chicken.
  4. Finish with lemon yoghurt and dill.
