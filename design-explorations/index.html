Shared principles

01

Action before information

The next useful move is always easier to find than the supporting explanation.

02

Plan and actual stay distinct

Recommendations, recordings, and AI assumptions use different visual language.

03

Depth on demand

Recipes, rationale, and editing tools are close by without dominating the default view.

04

Calm, honest feedback

Completion feels satisfying while skips, rest, and corrections remain normal and safe.
